## Locked Visual Direction

The production art direction is:

**Cozy Academy Storybook — High-Clarity Casual**

Visual rules:

- Warm, living and inviting football-academy world.
- Approximately 80% clean casual-game illustration and at most 20% restrained
  gouache or hand-painted texture.
- Soft 2.5D volume, large silhouettes and controlled academy-navy outlines.
- Shared upper-left lighting and one soft contact shadow.
- High readability on a phone-sized merge board.
- No pixel art, neon arcade treatment, rough doodles, plastic 3D, clay or anime
  styling.

The training-chain pilot is the reference implementation for future asset
conversion. Other chains retain their current assets until their own approved
conversion sprint.
