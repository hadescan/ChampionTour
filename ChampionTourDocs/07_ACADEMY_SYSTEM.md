# 07_ACADEMY_SYSTEM
## Football Academy
- First academy in the game.
- Academy progression is unlocked by Producer progression.
- Producer Lv6 triggers Renovation Mode.
### Renovation Stages
1. Old Field
2. Grass Upgrade
3. Goal Upgrade
4. Stands Upgrade
5. Lights
6. Decorations
7. Complete Football Academy
Rewards: Coins, XP, Diamonds.
After completion, Basketball Academy becomes available (future update).
